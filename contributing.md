# Contributing

We welcome contributions from students, club members, and cloud enthusiasts. You can easily participate and help improve this roadmap in several simple ways.

## How to Participate

1. **Join the Community:** The easiest way to get started is by joining our [AWS Cloud Computing WhatsApp Group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/FYpwSMmx2D76qt2eAM0vtF?s=sh&p=a&iam=2). You can ask questions, suggest new resources, share your progress, and collaborate with other members.
2. **Suggest Improvements:** Open a GitHub Issue to report broken links, suggest better tutorials, or propose updates for Phase 2 content.
3. **Submit Changes:** Fork this repository, add your improvements, and open a Pull Request for review.

## Contribution Guidelines

* Keep descriptions clear, concise, and beginner friendly.
* Prioritize free and accessible resources whenever possible.
* Ensure all external links are active and come from reliable sources.
